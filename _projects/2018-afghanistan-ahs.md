For the Afghanistan Health Survey 2018, I conducted the demographic and mortality analysis, estimated causes of death from verbal-autopsy data, and drafted the corresponding sections of the national report. The work translated household-survey records into population evidence that national partners could use to identify health priorities and inform program planning.
