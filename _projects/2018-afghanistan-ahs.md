I provided technical supported the Afghanistan Health Survey 2018 through demographic analysis, producing cause-of-death estimates from verbal autopsy surveys, and drafting corresponding sections in the country report. The work provides survey-based evidence identifying health priorities and guiding health program planning and policy making in the country.
