This technical assistance project supported verbal autopsy interviews and cause-of-death data collection in Guinea-Bissau. The work included planning for digital survey workflows, practical implementation support, and attention to how field data could move more reliably into analysis and reporting.

The project connected instrument design, field logistics, and data systems so that mortality information could be collected more consistently and used more effectively.
