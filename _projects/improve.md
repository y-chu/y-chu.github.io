From 2013 to 2018 at the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health, I worked on a connected series of projects that produced global, national, and subnational estimates of child mortality and its causes, including China and India, and presented this work at WHO and UN IGME technical meetings.

## Project lineage

- **CHERG** (Child Health Epidemiology Reference Group): evidence on maternal, neonatal, and child morbidity and mortality to inform global priorities. Graduate Research Assistant, 2013.
- **MCEE** (Maternal and Child Epidemiology Estimation): global, regional, national, and selected subnational estimates of maternal, neonatal, and child mortality. Research Associate, 2015–2017.
- **IMPROVE** (Improving Measurement and Program Design): estimates of causes and determinants of mortality and intervention coverage, supporting programming tools such as LiST. Research Associate, 2017–2018.
