CODA is a Gates Foundation initiative to improve cause-of-death assignment using artificial intelligence, coordinated through a global consortium led by Vital Strategies. It aims to strengthen death data in settings where reliable cause-of-death information is still limited.

My role centers on evaluating how well multimodal fusion approaches perform. I also take part in the consortium's discussions of model choice, development, and interpretation, contribute recommendations, and coauthor resulting papers.

## Project focus

- Improve cause-of-death assignment in low-resource settings
- Develop and test AI-enabled approaches for death data systems
- Support more reliable mortality evidence for policy and planning
