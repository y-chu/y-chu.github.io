This statewide research estimated the prevalence of current and past SARS-CoV-2 infection among Ohio adults when routine case counts could not provide a population-level measure of epidemic burden. My work integrated probability sampling, multiple diagnostic tests, Bayesian multilevel poststratification, and adjustment for nonresponse, sparse positives, and imperfect tests. The resulting studies were published in *PNAS* and *Annals of Epidemiology* and demonstrate substantive experience in infectious-disease surveillance as well as population-health methodology.
